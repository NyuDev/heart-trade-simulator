## Flag artwork

The two circular flags in `src/components/layout/FlagIcon.vue` are from the
**circle-flags** icon set by Harry Hutchinson, published under the **MIT**
licence.

- <https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags>

They are inlined rather than fetched so the interface needs no icon pipeline
for two images. They were not drawn here, and nothing in this project claims
authorship of them.
